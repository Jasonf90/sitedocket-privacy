SiteDocket Privacy Policy

SiteDocket: Job Photo Reports · Effective 8 November 2026

The short version: SiteDocket does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. There is no user account, no developer server, no third-party analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Every job, photo, voice note, service catalog item, signature, and PDF report stays locally on your device (and in your personal iCloud container if enabled).

What SiteDocket stores, and where

  • Jobs, Service Catalog & Business Profile. Your business name, logo, contact details, reusable service catalog, rates, client names, job addresses, itemized services, and notes are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Photos & Location Stamps. Before, During, and After job photos are saved in your app's local container on your device. If you grant Location access while using the camera, SiteDocket uses your device location strictly to reverse-geocode the street address onto photo timestamps and job headers. Location coordinates are never sent to any developer or third-party server.
  • Voice Notes & On-Device Transcription. Audio recordings are stored on your device and transcribed using Apple's on-device speech recognition and summarization frameworks. Your voice recordings and transcripts never leave your device.
  • PDF Reports & Signatures. Generated PDF reports and customer sign-off signatures are rendered on your device and shared only when you explicitly send them via Apple's system Share Sheet, Messages, or Mail.

Device permissions we request

  • Camera: To capture Before, During, and After job photos.
  • Photo Library: To import existing job photos or save stamped photos to your library when requested.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: To record and transcribe voice notes on your device so you don't have to type job summaries.
  • Location (While Using the App): Optional; used only to stamp the job address onto photos and reports.
  • Contacts: Optional; when you pick a client from your address book, only the selected client's name, phone, and email are copied into the job form.

Subscriptions & payments

SiteDocket Pro subscriptions are processed directly by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit 2). SiteDocket never receives or stores your credit card or billing details.

Exporting or deleting your data

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Open Settings › Export All Data in SiteDocket to export a full ZIP archive containing your jobs, CSV summary, service catalog, photos, audio notes, and PDF reports.
  • Delete any individual job, photo, voice note, or report inside the app, or uninstall SiteDocket to remove all local app data from your device.
