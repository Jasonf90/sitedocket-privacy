Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up my reusable Service Catalog & default tax rate?
Open Settings › Service Catalog & Pricing. Tap Load Starter Trade Services to pre-fill common trade items or tap + to add your own services, unit rates, and default sales tax percentage. On any job, tap Pick Service to itemize services in one tap.
How do I add a discount or adjust sales tax on a job?
Inside any job with itemized services, tap Add Tax or Discount (or Edit Tax & Discount) below the total cost to apply a dollar ($) or percentage (%) discount and customize the job's tax rate. Both appear automatically on your generated PDF report.
How do I back up or restore all of my jobs and photos?
Go to Settings › Export All Data to save a complete ZIP archive containing your jobs, service catalog, photos, voice notes, PDF reports, and CSV spreadsheet. To import a backup on a new device, tap Settings › Restore from Backup and select your ZIP file.
How do I restore my SiteDocket Pro subscription or manage billing?
Open Settings › Plan › Restore Purchases inside SiteDocket. To change or cancel a subscription, open the iOS Settings app › tap your Apple Account name › Subscriptions.
Are generated PDF reports, estimates, invoices, or receipts legally binding contracts?
No. Documents generated by SiteDocket may be used as job documentation, estimates, invoices, or receipts for informational and billing purposes, but they are not legally binding contracts, legal documents, or legal advice.
